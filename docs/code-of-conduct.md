# Code of conduct

LuckyBrain is an open-source project. Participation in project spaces is
expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and unwelcome conduct are not acceptable.

Be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas
and implementations rather than people; assume good intent while taking the
impact of words and actions seriously.

For concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
[security advisory channel](https://github.com/debashish-5/LuckyBrain/security/advisories/new)
or raise an issue asking for a private contact path. Do not post sensitive
personal information or security details publicly.

This summary complements the project-root
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/debashish-5/LuckyBrain/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
