# Code of Conduct

LuckyBrain is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

Expected behavior includes being considerate, giving and receiving feedback respectfully, focusing criticism on ideas and work, and helping keep discussions useful and inclusive.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. To report a concern, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's available contact or reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces.
